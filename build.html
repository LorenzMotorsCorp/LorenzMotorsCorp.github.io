Flight Club builds

Find the kit from this month's box and build along with the video. Grab a grown-up and build it together.

Test it in direct sunlight

The solar panel needs bright, direct sun to spin the motor. Indoor lights and cloudy days usually aren't strong enough, so take it outside for the first test.

  1. Mission 01

    Helicopter

    Build a solar helicopter and watch the main rotor spin up in the sun.

  2. Mission 02

    Solar car

    Build a car that drives itself on sunlight. No batteries, no charging.

  3. Mission 03

    Solar fan

    Build a fan and learn how a spinning blade moves air, the same idea that lifts a flying car.

  4. Mission 04

    Aeroboticar

    Build a model of the Lorenz Aeroboticar, the four fan flying car we're developing for real.
