HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. One afternoon a month.

1

Order the kit

Every month, a box shows up with a real machine to build: pre-cut wood parts, motor, solar panel, and a step-by-step guide.

2

Build it together

An afternoon at the table with a parent or grandparent. No screens, no batteries, just hands, focus, and the moment it moves on its own.

3

Watch engineering click

A blueprint card explains the real science behind each build, and connects it to how an actual flying car gets off the ground.

THE JOURNEY

From the ground, to the sky.

Each month builds on the last, the same way a flying car comes together: something that lifts, something that drives, something that moves air. Then in month 4, they build the flying car itself.

MONTH 1: LIFT

Solar helicopter

How spinning blades push air down and lift a machine up.

MONTH 2: POWER

Solar four-wheel racer

Turning sunlight into motion, and how a motor drives the wheels.

MONTH 3: AIRFLOW

Solar ducted fan

Why a ring around a fan makes it safer and stronger, the same idea behind the Aeroboticar.

MONTH 4: THE FLYING CAR

The Aeroboticar

Everything from months 1 to 3 comes together in a model of our real flying car. See the kit

MONTH 4: THE FLYING CAR

The Aeroboticar kit. Our flying car, on your kitchen table.

After lift, power and airflow comes the real thing. Every Flight Club member builds a laser-cut wooden model of the Aeroboticar, designed by the same team building the full-size flying car.

  • Four ducted fans that spin up on sunlight
  • LORENZ engraved on the hood
  • No batteries, ages 8 and up

Questions, answered.

What ages is Flight Club for?

Ages 8 and up. Younger builders do great with a grown-up at the table, and honestly, grown-ups tend to enjoy it too.

Do the kits need batteries?

No. Our builds run on solar power. Set them in the sun and watch them go.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Gift plans for 3, 6 or 12 months ship straight to your grandkid's door, with a card from you in the first box.

Are the kits safe?

Every kit is lab-tested to US toy safety standards before it ships.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks. No phone calls, no hoops.
